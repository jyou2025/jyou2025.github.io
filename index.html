About Me

I am a postdoctoral fellow working at the intersection of animal science, data science, mathematical modelling, and artificial intelligence.

My research focuses on applying machine learning, mechanistic models, and data-driven approaches to understand and improve animal production systems.

Research Interests

Precision Livestock Farming

Using sensor data, automated monitoring systems, and computational models to understand animal behaviour and production.

Machine Learning

Developing predictive models for animal production, feed manufacturing, and biological systems.

Mechanistic Modelling

Developing dynamic biological models using differential equations to describe growth, metabolism, and nutrient utilization.

Applied AI

Exploring AI agents and large language models for scientific research, data analysis, and decision support in animal science.

Selected Projects

Feed Mill PDI Prediction

Machine-learning approaches for predicting pellet durability across commercial feed production lines.

Methods: Python · Random Forest · Gradient Boosting · Support Vector Regression


Turkey Mechanistic Model

Development of a dynamic physiological model for turkey growth, nutrient utilization, and metabolism.

Methods: ODE modelling · Python · JAX · Bayesian inference


Mine Reclamation Ecosystem Recovery

Long-term analysis of vegetation, soil, and microbial recovery following reclamation treatments in boreal ecosystems.

Methods: Microbiome analysis · Ecological modelling · Structural Equation Modelling
